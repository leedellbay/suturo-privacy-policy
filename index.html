Suturo Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Who we are

Suturo is published by Lee Dell Bay Innovations LLC. Contact: leedellbay@gmail.com.

Summary

Suturo collects no data. Everything it does happens on your device. We do not receive, upload, store or share your files, your pages or anything about how you use the app.

Your files

Suturo reads only the PDFs and images you pick in the system file or photo picker; it cannot see anything else on your device. It never changes the originals. While you work, the pages are held in the app's memory. When you save, export or share, Suturo writes a new file where you choose, or hands it to the system share sheet, which sends it only where you send it. Copies the file picker or share sheet leaves in the app's temporary storage are cleared by the app.

Camera and document scanning

The camera is used only when you tap Scan, through your device's own document scanner (Apple's VisionKit on iPhone and iPad, Google's ML Kit document scanner from Google Play services on Android). The scan is made on your device, and Suturo receives only the finished page images. On Android, the scanner is part of Google Play services, which Google runs under its own privacy policy; Suturo itself has no network access on Android.

Passwords

A password you type to protect or open a PDF is used once, on your device, to encrypt or decrypt that file. It is never stored, logged or sent anywhere.

Information stored on your device

Only your settings are kept: the theme, the accent color, the view you last used and whether you have seen the app tour. Your documents are not kept between sessions. Removing the app removes the settings.

Network and third parties

Suturo does not use the network. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and no third party receives any information from the app.

Accounts

There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Children

Suturo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
